FAQ

Can solid hardwood be refinished?

Many solid hardwood floors can be sanded and refinished multiple times, depending on the plank profile, remaining wear layer and condition.

What should I plan for before installing solid hardwood?

Subfloor condition, moisture, acclimation requirements, plank dimensions, and transitions to adjacent rooms should be reviewed before installation. Material pricing is separate from removal, preparation, and labor.
