Durable choices for rentals

LVP

LVP is a common rental choice: it is waterproof as a plank, resists scratches, and damaged planks can often be replaced. A 20 mil wear layer is a sensible target for rental traffic.

Laminate

AC4-rated laminate is scratch-resistant and economical for bedrooms and living areas; keep it away from wet areas unless the product is rated for them.

Carpet

Stain-resistant carpet in bedrooms keeps units quieter for neighbors. A neutral color across units makes replacement easier.

Make repairs and future turnovers easier

  • Use one product and color across units where possible so leftover planks can patch damage later
  • Store a few boxes of spare flooring and matching trim
  • Choose mid-tone colors and textured finishes that hide wear and pet scratches
  • Use the same transitions and stair nosing details across units

Planning around vacancies

Turnovers are time-sensitive. Tell us when the unit will be vacant, the move-in date, the rooms involved, and any other trades (painting, cleaning) on the schedule. We confirm timing after reviewing removal, preparation and material availability.

Multi-family buildings and condos may have rules about work hours, parking, disposal and sound-rated underlayment. Share the building's requirements so they are part of the scope.

Questions

Rental & turnover flooring questions

What is the best flooring for a rental property?

Many owners choose LVP for kitchens, baths and living areas because it is waterproof as a plank and easy to repair, with durable carpet in bedrooms. The best fit depends on the unit and your budget.

Can you work around a tight turnover schedule?

Tell us the vacancy and move-in dates when you request a quote. We confirm timing after reviewing the scope and material availability.

Should I use the same flooring in every unit?

Using one product and color across units simplifies repairs, because spare planks from one job can patch another.

Do you work with property managers?

Property managers can request estimates the same way owners do. Include unit details, access instructions and building rules.
