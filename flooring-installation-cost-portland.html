Flooring installation cost in Portland: what goes into a quote

A flooring quote has more parts than the price on the box. This guide uses the material ranges from our Price Guide to show how a budget comes together, and explains the installation items that change from home to home.

The five parts of a flooring budget

  • Material: the flooring itself, plus a waste allowance for cuts and pattern matching
  • Installation supplies: underlayment or pad, vapor barrier, adhesive, fasteners, grout and thinset for tile
  • Removal and disposal: taking out the existing floor and hauling it away (flooring removal)
  • Preparation: fixing squeaks, flattening high and low spots, replacing damaged subfloor (subfloor prep and leveling)
  • Installation and finishing: laying the floor, plus baseboards, transitions and stairs

The Price Guide covers only the first part. The rest depends on your home, which is why we quote installation after reviewing your rooms.

What changes the installation side of a quote

Removal

Carpet comes up quickly; glued vinyl, nailed wood and mortar-set tile take much longer. Older resilient flooring and adhesives may need testing before removal.

Subfloor condition

Floating floors need a flat subfloor, wood floors need dry subfloors, and tile needs a stiff one. Some preparation is visible in photos; some only shows once the old floor is up.

Layout and rooms

Many small rooms, closets, hallways and doorways mean more cuts and transitions than one open room of the same size. Diagonal and herringbone layouts add labor and waste.

Stairs

Stairs are priced per step because each tread and riser is fit individually.

Furniture, appliances and access

Moving furniture, disconnecting appliances, stairs to the work area and parking all affect time on site.

Product requirements

Some products require specific underlayments, vapor barriers or adhesives to keep the manufacturer's warranty. We follow the manufacturer's installation instructions. Banana Floors installation and workmanship are covered by a 1-year warranty. Flooring materials are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, commonly about 10–25 years, and the length varies by product.

How to get an accurate quote

  • Measure each room (length × width) and note closets and hallways
  • Take photos of each room, doorways, the current floor and any stairs
  • Tell us the age of your home and what is under the current floor, if you know
  • Choose a material and tier in the Price Guide, or tell us you are undecided

Questions

Flooring cost questions

How much does flooring installation cost in Portland?

It depends on the material, the size of the job and the work around it: removal, subfloor preparation, stairs, trim and transitions. Use the Price Guide for material ranges and request a free preliminary quote for installation.

Why don't you list installation prices per square foot?

Installation cost varies widely with removal, subfloor condition, stairs, room layout and transitions. We review your rooms, measurements and photos and give a preliminary quote, then verify the scope on site.

How much extra flooring should I buy for waste?

A 10% allowance is common for straightforward rooms. Diagonal or herringbone layouts, many small rooms, or tile with lots of cuts may need 15%. Follow the manufacturer's guidance for your product.

Do the price ranges include tax and delivery?

No. The ranges are material-only planning estimates and exclude taxes, delivery, underlayment, adhesives, trim, transitions, removal, preparation and installation.

What's the cheapest flooring to install?

By material cost, laminate, entry-level carpet and entry-level LVP have the lowest ranges in our Price Guide. Total cost also depends on removal and preparation, which can matter more than the material.

Ready for a real number?

Send your rooms, approximate square footage, current flooring and a few photos. We review the scope, then verify measurements and site conditions before anything is scheduled.
