Service areas

Flooring installers for the Portland metro.

Banana Floors LLC is a licensed Oregon flooring contractor (CCB #209244) based in Portland.

We install flooring in Portland and nearby communities on both sides of the Willamette, plus Vancouver, Washington. Every project starts with the same questions: what is on the floor now, what is under it, and what you want the finished room to feel like. Older Portland bungalows, split-levels in Gresham and Beaverton, newer two-story homes in Happy Valley and condos on concrete slabs all need slightly different planning.

Each area page covers local housing, moisture and planning tips. For material budgets, start with the Price Guide; for everything else in a quote, see the installation cost guide.

FAQ

Flooring installation FAQ

What flooring do you install?

Hardwood, engineered wood, LVP, laminate, tile and carpet, along with removal and floor preparation when needed. See each flooring type for details.

Can you help with material selection and pricing?

Yes. Use our Price Guide for a planning range, then send us your project details and we can help narrow the options.

What areas does Banana Floors serve?

We provide flooring installation and replacement in Portland, Gresham, Happy Valley, Clackamas, Oregon City, Beaverton, Tigard and Lake Oswego, Oregon, and Vancouver, Washington. Call or text (503) 421-1730 to confirm your location.

Is Banana Floors a licensed contractor?

Yes. Banana Floors LLC holds Oregon Construction Contractors Board license CCB #209244.

Do you work on commercial projects?

Yes. We work on residential and commercial flooring projects. See commercial flooring.

Does the Price Guide include installation?

No. The Price Guide shows material-only planning ranges. Installation, removal, floor preparation, trim and taxes are quoted separately. Our cost guide explains what goes into a quote.
