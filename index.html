What flooring types do you work with?

Banana Floors works with common residential and commercial flooring products including hardwood, engineered wood, luxury vinyl plank and laminate, along with removal and floor-preparation work.

Yes. Removal and disposal can be included in the project scope when needed, along with basic floor preparation before installation.

Can you help me choose material?

Yes. The best choice depends on the space, traffic, moisture exposure, pets, maintenance expectations and budget.
