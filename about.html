About Banana Floors

About Banana Floors, a Portland flooring contractor

Banana Floors LLC is a Portland-area flooring installation company. We install hardwood, engineered wood, LVP, laminate, tile and carpet in homes and businesses across the Portland metro and Vancouver, Washington, with removal and floor preparation when needed.

How we work

Banana Floors is built around making flooring projects easier to plan. Most people shopping for new floors want two answers early: what the material will cost, and what the installation will involve. That is why the site includes a material Price Guide and a cost guide, and why every quote separates material from installation, removal and preparation.

  • Simple estimates: clear scope and realistic expectations
  • Detail-focused installation: clean cuts, transitions and final cleanup
  • Practical material guidance: options that fit the project and the budget
  • Verified before scheduling: measurements and site conditions are confirmed in person

Our process

  1. Choose and price: browse floors and check material ranges.
  2. Send details: square footage, photos and project location.
  3. Get a preliminary quote: we review your scope.
  4. Verify and confirm: measurements and site conditions.
  5. Schedule and install: we lock in the date and get it done.
